Publication Ethics

AJSD is committed to maintaining the highest standards of integrity, transparency, and ethical publishing.

Editorial Commitment

The Africa Journal of Sustainable Development (AJSD) is committed to publishing high-quality, original research that contributes to sustainable development scholarship and practice. Authors, reviewers, editors, and readers are expected to uphold the highest standards of academic integrity throughout the publication process.

The journal's editorial decisions are made independently and solely based on scholarly merit, originality, relevance to the journal's scope, methodological rigor, and contribution to knowledge.

Responsibilities of Authors

  • Submit only original work that has not been published or submitted elsewhere.
  • Ensure that all listed authors have made significant scholarly contributions.
  • Accurately report research methods, data, and findings.
  • Properly acknowledge the work of others through appropriate citation.
  • Disclose any financial or non-financial conflicts of interest.
  • Promptly notify the Editor if a significant error is discovered after submission or publication.

Originality and Plagiarism

Manuscripts submitted to AJSD must be original and must not contain plagiarism, duplicate publication, or substantial overlap with previously published work. All sources must be properly acknowledged.

Responsibilities of Reviewers

  • Provide objective, constructive, and timely reviews.
  • Maintain confidentiality of all submitted manuscripts.
  • Identify relevant published work that has not been cited.
  • Declare any conflict of interest before accepting a review assignment.

Responsibilities of Editors

  • Evaluate manuscripts solely on scholarly merit.
  • Maintain confidentiality throughout the editorial process.
  • Manage peer review fairly and impartially.
  • Take appropriate action in cases of suspected research misconduct.

Conflicts of Interest

Authors, reviewers, and editors should disclose any financial, institutional, professional, or personal relationships that could influence the objectivity of the publication process.

Research Ethics

Research involving human participants, animals, or other sensitive subjects should comply with applicable ethical standards and institutional or national regulations. Where required, authors should state that ethical approval was obtained from the appropriate ethics review board or committee and that informed consent was obtained from participants.

Data Availability

AJSD encourages authors to make research data, code, and other supporting materials available where appropriate to promote transparency and reproducibility. Where data cannot be shared, authors should provide a brief explanation of any legal, ethical, or confidentiality restrictions.

Use of Artificial Intelligence (AI)

Authors may use artificial intelligence (AI) tools to support language editing, data analysis, or other aspects of manuscript preparation where appropriate. Any material use of AI tools should be disclosed within the manuscript. Authors remain fully responsible for the accuracy, originality, integrity, and scientific content of their work. AI tools cannot be listed as authors.

Copyright

Upon acceptance, authors are required to complete and sign the journal's copyright agreement before publication.

Copyright © Centre for Sustainable Development, University of Ibadan, Nigeria.

Corrections and Retractions

Where significant errors or ethical concerns are identified after publication, AJSD may publish corrections, expressions of concern, or retractions where appropriate to preserve the integrity of the scholarly record.

Publication Malpractice

AJSD is committed to safeguarding the integrity of the scholarly record and does not tolerate publication malpractice in any form. This includes, but is not limited to:

  • Plagiarism or self-plagiarism.
  • Data fabrication or data falsification.
  • Duplicate or redundant publication.
  • Inappropriate authorship or omission of deserving authors.
  • Citation manipulation or deliberate misrepresentation of sources.
  • Undisclosed conflicts of interest.
  • Peer review manipulation or interference with the editorial process.

Where publication malpractice is suspected, the Editorial Board will investigate the matter fairly and confidentially. Confirmed cases may result in rejection of the manuscript, publication of a correction or retraction, notification of the authors' institution or funding agency, where appropriate, and other actions necessary to protect the integrity of the journal.

Ethics Enquiries

Questions regarding publication ethics or allegations of research misconduct should be directed to the Editor.
