Featured Event

Ibadan Sustainable Development Summit (ISDS)

The Ibadan Sustainable Development Summit (ISDS) is an annual event held each August that brings together researchers, policymakers, development practitioners, students, and other stakeholders to discuss emerging issues and practical solutions for sustainable development in Africa.

Information about upcoming editions of the Summit, including themes, keynote speakers, and registration details, will be announced as they become available.
