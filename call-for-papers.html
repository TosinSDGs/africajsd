Areas of Interest

AJSD welcomes high-quality research that advances knowledge, policy, and practice in sustainable development, particularly within Africa and other developing regions.

  • Sustainable development policy and practice
  • Climate change and environmental sustainability
  • Natural resource management
  • Economic development and inclusive growth
  • Social development and poverty reduction
  • Food security and agriculture
  • Public health and community development
  • Governance, institutions, and public policy
  • Education, innovation, and technology for sustainable development
