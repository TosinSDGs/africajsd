Author Guidelines

Guidance for preparing and submitting manuscripts to the Africa Journal of Sustainable Development.

Before You Submit

The journal publishes original, peer-reviewed research that advances sustainable development in Africa and beyond. Authors should ensure that manuscripts comply with the journal's scope, formatting requirements, and ethical standards before submission.

Nature of Articles

  • The Africa Journal of Sustainable Development publishes original research articles, research notes, review articles, policy and practice reports, book reviews, publication announcements, and reports from sustainable development agencies and institutions. Manuscripts must be written in English and must not have been previously published or be under consideration elsewhere.

Manuscripts must be written in English and should not have been published or submitted elsewhere.

Eligibility to Contribute

AJSD welcomes submissions from members of the African Sustainable Development Network, researchers, academics, policymakers, development practitioners, and professionals working on sustainable development worldwide.

Manuscript Preparation

Language English
Maximum Length 15,000 words
Line Spacing 1.5
Paper Size A4
Margins Minimum 1.5 inches
Units SI Units

Pages should be numbered consecutively. Use title case for primary headings and avoid numbering section headings. Scientific names should be italicized, and chemical names should be written in full rather than as chemical formulae where appropriate.

Title

The manuscript title should be concise, written in title case, and should not exceed 23 words.

Abstract

Each manuscript should include a single-paragraph abstract of no more than 250 words summarizing the study, methodology, principal findings, and conclusions.

Keywords

Provide up to five keywords immediately following the abstract.

Tables and Figures

  • Number tables consecutively using Arabic numerals.
  • Number figures consecutively.
  • Insert tables and figures close to where they are first cited.
  • Use only high-quality figures and photographs.
  • Avoid duplicating information in both tables and figures.

References

References should be current, relevant, and presented in alphabetical order by the surname of the first author. Journal titles should be italicized, volume numbers should appear in bold, and complete page ranges should be provided. 


Bryant, C.L. & Proctor, J. (1970).
Rainfall distribution of a growth indicator for
Pterocarpus angolensis in Tanzania.
Commonwealth Forestry Review, 46, 180–184.

Proofs

Authors will receive one proof for review before publication. Corrections should be limited to typographical or factual errors. Extensive revisions at the proof stage may incur additional costs.

Publication Fee

Authors of accepted manuscripts are required to pay a publication fee of USD 100 per manuscript. One complimentary copy of the journal will be provided. Additional copies may be purchased separately.

Manuscript Submission

AJSD currently accepts manuscript submissions by email. An online submission system is under development and will be introduced in a future release of the journal website.

Submission Checklist

  • ✓ The manuscript falls within the journal's scope.
  • ✓ The manuscript is original and has not been published or submitted elsewhere.
  • ✓ The manuscript is written in English.
  • ✓ The manuscript does not exceed 15,000 words.
  • ✓ A title (maximum 23 words), abstract (maximum 250 words), and up to five keywords are included.
  • ✓ Tables and figures are properly numbered and cited in the text.
  • ✓ References are complete, accurate, and formatted according to the journal's guidelines.
  • ✓ The manuscript is ready for submission as a Microsoft Word document.
