Volume 4 • Issue 3

2014

Volume 4, Issue 3 (2014) is a Special Issue on Higher Education and Sustainable Development in Africa. The issue contains 12 contributions examining the role of higher education, sustainability science, urban development, mining, governance, innovation, and regional partnerships in advancing sustainable development across Africa.
