Editorial Board

The Africa Journal of Sustainable Development is supported by an international Editorial Board comprising distinguished scholars, researchers, and practitioners with expertise across sustainable development, environmental sciences, economics, public health, governance, forestry, and related disciplines.

About the Editorial Board

The Editorial Board provides academic leadership for the journal, ensuring that published manuscripts meet high standards of scientific quality, originality, and relevance to sustainable development. Board members contribute to editorial policy, support the peer-review process, and promote excellence in interdisciplinary research.

Role in the Peer Review Process

The Editorial Board conducts an initial assessment of submitted manuscripts to determine their suitability for the journal's aims and scope. Suitable manuscripts are assigned to independent reviewers with expertise in the relevant field. Reviewers evaluate submissions for originality, methodological rigor, scholarly contribution, and relevance before editorial decisions are made.

International Representation

The Editorial Board reflects the journal's commitment to interdisciplinary scholarship and international collaboration. Members represent universities and research institutions across Africa and North America, bringing expertise from diverse academic disciplines that support sustainable development research.

Contributing to the journal

The journal welcomes collaboration with researchers, reviewers, and practitioners committed to advancing sustainable development research. Experts interested in serving as peer reviewers are encouraged to contact the Editorial Office.
